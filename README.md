# OctaRoll - Next-Gen 2-8 Player Ludo

Welcome to the official releases repository for **OctaRoll**, the 2 to 8 player Ludo board game experience for Android.

## Key Features
- **2 to 8 Players**: Play the classic 4-player cross board or expand to 5, 6, 7, and 8-player radial polygonal arenas.
- **3D Upright Pawns**: Sculpted 3D pawns with tabletop drop-shadows, metallic pedestals, and specular highlights.
- **True 3D Isometric Dice**: Multi-face isometric 3D cube with perspective pips, realistic rolling tumble, and ground impact physics.
- **Modular Dice Shop**: Collect exclusive dice themes like Diya Festive (Diwali Special), Cyber Neon, Imperial Jade, and Champion 24K Gold.
- **Multiple Game Modes**:
  - **Pass and Play**: Local multiplayer on a single device.
  - **Vs Computer**: Offline AI practice with 4 difficulty levels.
  - **Online Multiplayer**: Worldwide matchmaking and private custom rooms.
- **Daily Lucky Bonus**: Claim daily reward coins every 24 hours.

## Downloads and Installation
Download the latest APK asset from the **[Releases](https://github.com/a092devs/octaroll-releases/releases)** section:

1. Download `OctaRoll-v1.0.0.apk`.
2. On your Android device, tap the file and select **Install** (allow *Install from Unknown Sources* if prompted).
3. Open the game and roll the dice!

---

## Minimum Requirements
- **OS**: Android 8.0 (API level 26) or higher.
- **RAM**: 2 GB minimum (4 GB recommended).
- **Supported ABIs**: arm64-v8a, armeabi-v7a, x86_64.
